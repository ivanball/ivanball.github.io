    Architecture governance

    MMCA.ADC: Architecture Remediation Backlog

    Derived from ArchitectureScorecard.md (evidence as of 2026-10-22 at MMCA.Common.* v1.250.0): Maturity 97.5% (316/324) / Implementation 86.0% (697/810), weight total 81, no N/A category. Work is ranked on both scorecard axes, one band per axis. Maturity band: every category scoring maturity < 4, ranked by (4 - maturity) x weight. Implementation band: every category scoring implementation <= 8, ranked by max(0, 9 - implementation) x weight (target 9: a 10 is awardable at re-score time for an almost perfect implementation, but ranking against 10 would drown the real gaps, so 9 governs scheduling). Ties break by weight descending, then category number. Today 3 categories sit in the maturity band (8 weighted points), 14 in the implementation band (32 gap points), 31 score maturity 4 (protect, do not regress) and 19 of those also score implementation >= 9, which is the only combination that is done on both axes. This is the single remediation and tech-debt ledger: each deferred sub-item keeps its TD-NN ID with its blocker, resolution path and effort. Effort key: S = hours · M = about 1 day · L = multi-day. Right-size every fix to the measured conference load (~76 accounts / ~67 peak concurrent).
