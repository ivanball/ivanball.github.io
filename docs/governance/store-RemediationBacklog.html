    Architecture governance

    MMCA.Store: Architecture Remediation Backlog

    Derived from ArchitectureScorecard.md: Maturity 96.8% (306/316) / Implementation 83.2% (657/790), evidence as of 2026-11-23 at MMCA.Common.* v1.315.0 (lockstep, HEAD c0237af6, clean tree). Items are ranked on both scorecard axes, one band per axis. The maturity band holds categories scoring maturity < 4, ranked by priority = (4 − maturity) × weight. The implementation band holds categories scoring implementation <= 8**, ranked by **implPriority = max(0, 9 − implementation) × weight**; the scheduling target is **9, not 10**, because a 10 is recognition earned at re-score time rather than scheduled work, and ranking against 10 would put nearly every strong category in the band and drown the real gaps. Ties break by weight descending, then category number. A category leaves each band independently and reaches the protect list only at **maturity 4 AND implementation >= 9; the indices keep their × 4 and × 10 denominators, and the 9-target governs scheduling only.

    Facts re-verified 2026-11-24 against MMCA.Common v1.318.0 (Store HEAD 7875dfd8); scores, indices and bands are unchanged from the evidence date above and move only at the next re-score.

Tactical deferrals are recorded here as TD sub-items under their category (the single-ledger model). Status: [ ] open, [~] under way, [x] resolved.
