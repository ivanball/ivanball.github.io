    MMCA.Store: Architecture Remediation Backlog

    Derived from ArchitectureScorecard.md: Maturity 97.5% (308/316) / Implementation 83.2% (657/790), evidence as of 2026-10-22 at MMCA.Common.* v1.250.0 (lockstep, HEAD d8d3ac91, clean tree). Items are ranked on both scorecard axes, one band per axis. The maturity band holds categories scoring maturity < 4, ranked by priority = (4 − maturity) × weight. The implementation band holds categories scoring implementation <= 8**, ranked by **implPriority = max(0, 9 − implementation) × weight**; the scheduling target is **9, not 10**, because a 10 is recognition earned at re-score time rather than scheduled work, and ranking against 10 would put nearly every strong category in the band and drown the real gaps. Ties break by weight descending, then category number. A category leaves each band independently and reaches the protect list only at **maturity 4 AND implementation >= 9; the indices keep their × 4 and × 10 denominators, and the 9-target governs scheduling only.

    The scorecard is the source of truth for scores, ../MMCA.Common/FACTS.md for framework-wide facts, and the workspace-internal Docs/Architecture/ArchitectureRemediation.md (not published) for the cross-repo [C→A] roll-up. Tactical deferrals are recorded here as TD sub-items under their category (the single-ledger model). Status: [ ] open, [~] under way, [x] resolved.

Next: check the deployed Store Identity database for accounts seeded by builds that predate the gate (Store PR #124) and rotate or remove them (user-side; not provable from the repo).
