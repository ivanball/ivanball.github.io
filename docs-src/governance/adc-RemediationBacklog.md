Work is ranked on both scorecard axes, one band per axis. **Maturity band:** every category scoring maturity < 4, ranked by `(4 - maturity) x weight`. **Implementation band:** every category scoring implementation <= 8, ranked by `max(0, 9 - implementation) x weight` (target 9: a 10 is awardable at re-score time for an almost perfect implementation, but ranking against 10 would drown the real gaps, so 9 governs scheduling). Ties break by weight descending, then category number. Today 4 categories sit in the maturity band (10 weighted points), 14 in the implementation band (34 gap points), 30 score maturity 4 (protect, do not regress) and 19 of those also score implementation >= 9, which is the only combination that is done on both axes.
