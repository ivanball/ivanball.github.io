# MMCA.Store: Architecture Remediation Backlog

Derived from [`ArchitectureScorecard.md`](../governance/store-ArchitectureScorecard.md): **Maturity 97.5% (308/316) / Implementation 83.2% (657/790)**, evidence as of 2026-10-18 at MMCA.Common.* v1.244.0 (lockstep, HEAD `b40baf14`, clean tree). Items are ranked on both scorecard axes, one band per axis. The **maturity band** holds categories scoring **maturity < 4**, ranked by **priority = (4 − maturity) × weight**. The **implementation band** holds categories scoring **implementation <= 8**, ranked by **implPriority = max(0, 9 − implementation) × weight**; the scheduling target is **9, not 10**, because a 10 is recognition earned at re-score time rather than scheduled work, and ranking against 10 would put nearly every strong category in the band and drown the real gaps. Ties break by weight descending, then category number. A category leaves each band independently and reaches the protect list only at **maturity 4 AND implementation >= 9**; the indices keep their `× 4` and `× 10` denominators, and the 9-target governs scheduling only.

The scorecard is the source of truth for scores, [`../MMCA.Common/FACTS.md`](https://github.com/ivanball/MMCA.Common/blob/main/FACTS.md) for framework-wide facts, and the workspace-internal `Docs/Architecture/ArchitectureRemediation.md` (not published) for the cross-repo `[C→A]` roll-up. Tactical deferrals are recorded here as `TD` sub-items under their category (the single-ledger model). Status: `[ ]` open, `[~]` under way, `[x]` resolved.
