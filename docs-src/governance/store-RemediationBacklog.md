Items are ranked on both scorecard axes, one band per axis. The **maturity band** holds categories scoring **maturity < 4**, ranked by **priority = (4 − maturity) × weight**. The **implementation band** holds categories scoring **implementation <= 8**, ranked by **implPriority = max(0, 9 − implementation) × weight**; the scheduling target is **9, not 10**, because a 10 is recognition earned at re-score time rather than scheduled work, and ranking against 10 would put nearly every strong category in the band and drown the real gaps. Ties break by weight descending, then category number. A category leaves each band independently and reaches the protect list only at **maturity 4 AND implementation >= 9**; the indices keep their `× 4` and `× 10` denominators, and the 9-target governs scheduling only.

The scorecard is the source of truth for scores, [`../MMCA.Common/FACTS.md`](https://github.com/ivanball/MMCA.Common/blob/main/FACTS.md) for framework-wide facts, and the workspace-internal `Docs/Architecture/ArchitectureRemediation.md` (not published) for the cross-repo `[C→A]` roll-up. Tactical deferrals are recorded here as `TD` sub-items under their category (the single-ledger model). Status: `[ ]` open, `[~]` under way, `[x]` resolved.

- **Closed as over-engineering.** Each was weighed against one developer and about 67 peak users; none has a defect behind it that an existing gate misses, so each would add permanent machinery for a score point.   - **#11 Data Protection wrap of the cached Stripe signing secret** (user decision): the Sales host registers no shared key ring, so the wrap would first need key-ring infrastructure, for a secret whose only exposure is read access to Redis.
  - **#10 real-broker round-trips for `CustomerErased`/`ProductInfoChanged` and an ordering declaration for `ProductVariantChanged`:** the contract snapshot and the required `[EventName]` rule cover the wire-name failure; the ordering race is theoretical at this load.
  - **#17 Bicep what-if before `arm-deploy`, and probe-only rollback:** what-if needs Azure credentials in pull-request workflows, `arm-deploy` already fails on bad Bicep, and ADR-080 keeps skip-live.
  - **#20 StoreHome palette tokenization:** cosmetic; the dark-palette axe scan passes and the brand token is already guarded.
  - **#22 density toggle and coarse-pointer touch-target CSS:** no shopper need; the phone-viewport E2E now guards the shopper pages.

  - **Rows with no recorded lever (#18, #19, #23, #30, #31, #33):** #18 is under the cap, which forces a split when due (the shared field blocks are in place, see Resolved); #19's client cache was withdrawn after staleness failures; #23's product-image derivatives are a storage redesign while the browse CWV budgets pass; #30's consent capture has no processing that needs consent (no marketing mail); #31 and #33 are recorded postures below.
