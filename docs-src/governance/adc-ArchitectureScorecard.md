# MMCA.ADC: Architecture Scorecard

> **Canonical, version-controlled scorecard for this repo**: the single source of truth for MMCA.ADC's architecture scores, scored against the rubric at [`ArchitectureEvaluationCriteria.md`](ArchitectureEvaluationCriteria.md); framework-wide facts in [`../MMCA.Common/FACTS.md`](https://github.com/ivanball/MMCA.Common/blob/main/FACTS.md). Remediation lives in [`RemediationBacklog.md`](../governance/adc-RemediationBacklog.md), the single remediation and tech-debt ledger (it carries the tactical `TD-NN` items); the cross-repo comparison lives in the workspace-internal `Docs/Architecture/CrossRepoComparison.md` (not published).

_Evidence as of 2026-10-07 at MMCA.Common.* v1.233.0, git HEAD `665b2d3c` (clean tree). Rubric version 2._

## At a glance

- **Maturity index** = Σ(maturity×weight) ÷ Σ(weight×4) = 314 ÷ 324 = **96.9%**
- **Implementation index** = Σ(impl×weight) ÷ Σ(weight×10) = 697 ÷ 810 = **86.0%**
- **Weaker axis:** Implementation, 10.9 points below Maturity.
- **Maturity band:** 4 categories sit below maturity 4 (Sections 10, 12, 21 and 22; 10 weighted points), ranked in the [backlog](../governance/adc-RemediationBacklog.md).
- **Implementation band:** 14 categories sit at implementation 8 or below (34 weighted points short of 9 on every category), ranked in the [backlog](../governance/adc-RemediationBacklog.md).

MMCA.ADC is a .NET 10.0 DDD / Clean Architecture system for the Atlanta Developers Conference, run as four independently hosted services (Identity, Conference, Engagement, Notification) behind a YARP gateway, collaborating over Result-over-the-wire gRPC and MassTransit integration events on the outbox, one database per service, on the lockstep `MMCA.Common.*` packages (20 of the 22 published packages, `Directory.Packages.props:111`). Its strongest trait is that architectural intent is executable: 54 architecture-test classes gate CI on rules inherited from the shared `MMCA.Common.Testing.Architecture` library, and recovery, capacity, cost and cross-browser proofs are deploy preconditions sized to measured conference load (~67 peak). The remaining weight sits on the implementation axis, led by the responsive substance of §22 (implPriority 4), and on four maturity gaps: §10's broker faults have no alert or replay procedure, §12's capacity proof runs out of band, §21's manual screen-reader pass is unrecorded, and §22's phone-width layout is guarded by review rather than by a test.

## Top 5 risks

1. **Responsive behavior holds the lowest implementation score and sits in both bands:** §22 (mat 3 / impl 7, weight 2; the top row of the implementation band at implPriority 4, and in the maturity band at priority 2). Cross-engine recency is gated (`cross-browser-freshness`, `deploy.yml:911`, in `deploy.needs` `:1147`, hard condition `:1181`), but that is the only automated check: no ADC test sets a phone viewport and no responsive fitness test exists, so layout, reflow and density are enforced by review. Both implementation deductions hold: the rubric's density-options criterion has zero adoption in ADC although the framework ships it (`DataGridListPageBase.cs:78` `DenseGrid`, `:909` `ToggleDensity()`; `EventList.razor:73` hardcodes `Dense="true"`), and 20 `.razor` files render raw tables outside the DataGrid's grid-to-card degradation; 12 of them are key/value detail tables that wrap on mobile (`adc-ui.css:33`), while the session-selection statistics tables use fixed-width columns with no mobile alternative (`adc-ui.css:80`).
   - _Remediation:_ Adopt the density toggle on the DataGrid list pages (bind `DenseGrid` / `ToggleDensity()` instead of the hardcoded `Dense`), give the selection-dashboard and organizer tables a card or stacked degradation at the phone breakpoint, and add an E2E or fitness check that asserts layout at a phone viewport (effort M).
   - _Expected:_ An automated phone-viewport check lifts §22 maturity 3→4; density adoption lifts impl 7→8, and reflow on every raw-table surface lifts it 8→9.

2. **The manual screen-reader pass is unrecorded:** §21 (mat 3 / impl 8, weight 3). The chromium E2E/axe suite blocks the deploy (`e2e-gate` in `deploy.needs`) and cross-browser recency is gated (`cross-browser-freshness`, `deploy.yml:911`, in `deploy.needs` `:1147`), but the recorded screen-reader pass that the rubric's maturity-4 bar asks for is still the empty placeholder row in `adc-ACCESSIBILITY-SCREENREADER-PASS.md:64`.
   - _Remediation:_ Record the manual screen-reader pass (needs a human with NVDA or VoiceOver).
   - _Expected:_ §21 maturity 3→4 (weight 3).

3. **Broker faults have no operational path:** §10 (mat 3 / impl 8, weight 3). `FaultIntegrationEventConsumer.cs:57` logs "faulted ... moved to the error queue", but the only messaging log alert matches the outbox's "dead-lettered" text (`infra/main.bicep:579`), so no alert covers broker faults; ADR-087 records no alert, runbook or replay path (`../adr/087-broker-poison-message-handling.md:202-204`). The contract snapshot spells out generic arguments and is keyed by the `[EventName]` wire identity (Common `ArchitectureRules.Events.cs:45,:82`; `IntegrationEventContractTests.cs:17`). The missing ordering stamp on the speaker-link handlers is an accepted limit recorded in the code (`SpeakerLinkedToUserHandler.cs:25-27`).
   - _Remediation:_ An alert plus runbook for the broker error queue with a replay procedure, wired as alert-as-code.
   - _Expected:_ The alert, runbook and replay procedure lift §10 impl 8→9; alert-as-code for broker faults and an `[EventName]` requirement on every event are the maturity 3→4 levers.

4. **SQL data plane: public network access remains:** §11 (mat 4 / impl 8, weight 3) and §17 (mat 4 / impl 9). App-to-database auth is passwordless managed identity in production, with the SQL admin login kept only as a dormant fallback. The residual is network exposure: `infra/main.bicep:835` `publicNetworkAccess: 'Enabled'` plus the AllowAzureServices firewall rule at `:843-844`, both deferred by design (no VNet; documented-accepted in `infra/SQL-MANAGED-IDENTITY.md`). §11's other v2 deduction, the missing threat model, is a recorded decision not to schedule it.
   - _Remediation:_ Move the SQL data plane onto private endpoints (disable public network access, drop the 0.0.0.0 rule). This is the VNet and private-endpoint epic, which requires recreating the Container Apps environment, hence deferred.
   - _Expected:_ Closing public network access is the 9→10 rung for §17; §11 holds at 8 until a lever other than the unscheduled threat model is named.

5. **The API contract guard cannot catch a breaking change, the capacity proof runs out of band, and the MAUI head sits outside CI:** §9 (mat 4 / impl 8), §12 (mat 3 / impl 9) and §15 (mat 4 / impl 8). The shared OpenAPI guard is well-formedness plus a paths-count floor and pinned resource strings, not a baseline diff (`MMCA.Common.Testing/Conformance/OpenApiContractTestsBase.cs:38,:48,:62`), the committed per-service OpenAPI documents' drift gate (`deploy.yml:305-320`) catches an uncommitted change but does not classify a breaking one, and no AsyncAPI-style async contract document exists. The k6 tier executes on a monthly cron plus dispatch (`load-test.yml:23`) and `load-freshness` gates on recency, not execution (`deploy.yml:851`, `:860`, `:1147`). The MAUI `MMCA.ADC.UI` project is in no CI build (`MMCA.ADC.CI.slnf:25,:26` list only the web heads), so its analyzers and its own `NoWarn CA5392` (`MMCA.ADC.UI.csproj:155`) are review-only.
   - _Remediation:_ Commit an OpenAPI baseline per service, diff the live document against it in the integration tier, and publish the seven integration-event contracts as an AsyncAPI-style document (TD-24, effort M); run the k6 tier (or a scaled-down smoke of it) inside the deploy chain; bring the MAUI head inside a CI build (TD-18), verified by a full-solution package-mode restore.
   - _Expected:_ The contract diff plus async document lifts §9 impl 8→9; an in-band capacity proof lifts §12 maturity 3→4; MAUI inside a CI build lifts §15 impl 8→9.

   - _Expected:_ §34 impl 9→10.

2. **Disaster recovery is drilled and gated, graceful shutdown is CI-verified, and the privacy residency claim matches the deployment** (§29 mat 4 / impl 9, §30 mat 4 / impl 9): `infra/DISASTER-RECOVERY.md:212-217` records six PITR restore proofs across all four live databases (the newest restores `ADC_Conference` in 2.1 min against the 2 h RTO); the `dr-freshness` job fails the deploy unless the latest `dr-drill.yml` run succeeded within an 8-day window (`deploy.yml:832`, `window-days` at `:841`, in `deploy.needs` at `:1147`), and the drill job itself runs under a 60-minute timeout (`dr-drill.yml:49`, rationale `:42-48`); `Tests/Hosts/MMCA.ADC.Gateway.Tests/GracefulShutdownTests.cs` (a sealed subclass of `MMCA.Common.Testing/GracefulShutdownTestsBase.cs`) CI-verifies a bounded drain; `PRIVACY.md:53,61` reads East US 2 / West US 2 and is guarded by `DataResidencyTests`.

   - _Remediation:_ Replace `style-src 'unsafe-inline'` with the framework's nonce path and narrow `img-src https:` to specific origins.
   - _Expected:_ §26 impl 9→10.

4. **Capacity and FinOps are data-driven from measured conference load** (§31 mat 4 / impl 8, §12 mat 3 / impl 9): `conference-read-load.js:56-57` (p95<800 ms, <1% errors; 67 VUs, the observed peak, on manual dispatch, `load-test.yml:18`, and 40 VUs on the scheduled run, `:75`), a green proof on the deployed 0.25 vCPU / 0.5 Gi topology (run 35013811738, `infra/main.bicep:1719`) whose recency gates the deploy (`load-freshness`, `deploy.yml:851`); `cost-guard.yml` in `deploy.needs` (`deploy.yml:771`, `:1147`, condition `:1180`) fails the deploy if maxReplicas>2 or any SQL DB tier!=Basic (`cost-guard.yml:59-61`, `:74-76`); a monthly RG budget with 80%/100% alerts (`main.bicep:793`); a per-service `service` cost tag on the six container apps and the four databases (`main.bicep:1679`, `:965`); a 5 GB/day Log Analytics ingestion cap sized as a runaway guard (`foundation.bicep:15`, applied at `:53-55`).
   - _Remediation:_ Convert the conference-day surge and revert from a manual play with a drift alarm into a scheduled, automated scale event with an automatic revert.
   - _Expected:_ §31 impl 8→9.

5. **Non-inverted test pyramid with deploy-gated integration, E2E and markup-snapshot tiers** (§14 mat 4 / impl 9, §28 mat 4 / impl 9): 1507 unit/UI `[Fact]/[Theory]` across 223 files; 303 gating integration methods across four per-service WAF tiers; 12 markup-snapshot `[Fact]`s over 12 committed `.html` baselines in all three bUnit projects (`ComponentsSnapshotTests.cs:27`), failing on a missing baseline.    - _Expected:_ §28 impl 9→10.
