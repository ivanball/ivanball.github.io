# MMCA.Common: Architecture Scorecard

> **Canonical, version-controlled scorecard for this repo**: the single source of truth for MMCA.Common's architecture scores. Scored against the rubric at [`ArchitectureEvaluationCriteria.md`](ArchitectureEvaluationCriteria.md) (rubric version 2, [ADR-110](../adr/110-rubric-v2-category-realignment.md)); framework-wide facts (version, packages, ADR range, fitness counts) live in [`FACTS.md`](https://github.com/ivanball/MMCA.Common/blob/main/FACTS.md); the ranked open work lives in the [remediation backlog](common-RemediationBacklog.md). The cross-repo Common-vs-ADC-vs-Store narrative lives once in the workspace-internal `Docs/Architecture/CrossRepoComparison.md` (not published).

_Evidence as of 2026-09-22 at v1.209.0, git HEAD `0527211` (clean tree). Rubric version 2._

## At a glance

- **Maturity index** = Σ(maturity×weight) ÷ Σ(weight×4) = 320 ÷ 328 = **97.6%**
- **Implementation index** = Σ(impl×weight) ÷ Σ(weight×10) = 711 ÷ 820 = **86.7%**
- **Weaker axis:** Implementation (execution quality), by ~10.9 points.
- **Maturity band:** 3 categories sit below Maturity 4 (§31 at 2, §17 and §30 at 3), 8 weighted gap points; see the [backlog](common-RemediationBacklog.md).
- **Implementation band:** 12 categories sit at Implementation 8 or below (§4, §5, §11, §12, §13, §17, §23, §24, §29, §30, §31, §34), 27 weighted gap points against the scheduling target of 9; see the [backlog](common-RemediationBacklog.md).

MMCA.Common is strongest where its conventions are executable: 31 of 34 categories sit at Maturity 4, most of them on build-breaking gates (dual-enforced layering, fitness functions shipped as a package and re-run in every consumer, required cross-browser, accessibility, performance and consumer-canary checks, a CI-gated `FACTS.md`). The remaining gap sits on the Implementation axis, and most of its weight is structural rather than neglected: deployment execution, production SLOs, dashboards, load testing, cost right-sizing and the privacy process belong to the consuming apps, which is why §17, §29, §30 and §31 hold at 8 or below. The in-repo levers left are small and named in the backlog (a sample dashboard, front-end payload budgets, form parity and localization, the workspace architecture map).

## Top 5 risks

1. **FinOps execution and governance live downstream**, §31 (impl 8, maturity 2, the lowest maturity on the board; a documented accepted cap per the backlog's *Deliberate and accepted* section): the framework ships real cost levers (an OTel `Telemetry:TracesSampleRatio` sampler knob, the outbox per-message log at Debug, `OutboxPollFilterProcessor`, and the default-ON probe-telemetry filter plus its span processor, `Aspire/Extensions.cs:235,257`, that keep health-probe traces and their SQL/Redis children out of ingestion), but right-sizing, reversible scale-events and per-service cost attribution are consumer/IaC concerns a library cannot perform, and no in-repo process or review enforces cost discipline.
   - _Remediation:_ The in-repo telemetry-cost half is done. What remains is structural: the consumer apps must demonstrate measured right-sizing and per-service cost attribution that the framework's knobs feed, and a cost convention would have to be enforced somewhere (nothing under `Tests/Architecture` or in any workflow gates cost).
   - _Expected delta:_ +1 maturity on §31 (2→3, +2 weighted) needs an enforced cost convention; 8→9 needs the consumer-side right-sizing and attribution evidence.

2. **Deployment gating is verified only downstream**, §17 (maturity 3, impl 8, weight 2): a reference `samples/deployment` Bicep/OIDC sample + `DEPLOYMENT.md` ship and are compile-checked on every pull request, but that job is not a required context, and a library cannot self-deploy, so real CD-to-Azure lives in consumers; keyless nuget.org publishing under ADR-053 and the reviewer-gated `release` environment harden the release identity, not the deploy axis.
   - _Remediation:_ A smoke-deploy of the `samples/deployment` sample, which moves §17 from referenced toward proven.
   - _Expected delta:_ +1 impl on §17 (+2 weighted), and the maturity rung, remain available downstream.

3. **Compliance governance is consumer-owned; the in-repo guard verifies a mechanism, not the category**, §30 (impl 8, maturity 3, weight 2): the headline §30 red flags are closed in-framework (the `IAnonymizable` erasure extension point, AES-256-GCM at rest, and the shipped, tested `PiiRedactor` masking `[Pii]` members before logs/telemetry), and `PiiErasureContractFitnessTests` forces a `[Pii]` type through `PiiRedactor` + `IAnonymizable`. That gate verifies the *mechanism* rather than a repo-wide convention (the structural `PiiConventionTests` scan is vacuous because no PII-bearing type lives in Common's Domain), and the governing *process* (the personal-data inventory, consent capture, residency verification) is delegated to consumers, so the category as a whole is not automatically enforced.
   - _Remediation:_ The in-repo mechanism is done; further maturity movement needs the consumer apps to carry the personal-data inventory + DSAR/consent/residency process the framework's `[Pii]`/`IAnonymizable` extension points feed (ADR-005).
   - _Expected delta:_ +1 maturity on §30 (3→4, +2 weighted) is a consumer-side governance lift, not an in-repo one; the framework half is complete.

4. **Security holds at Implementation 8 on criteria that are unmet by decision**, §11 (maturity 4, impl 8, weight 3, implPriority 3): authentication, authorization, rate limiting, brute-force protection, PBKDF2 hashing and security headers are strong and centralized, but the rubric-v2 written threat model is absent repo-wide and is recorded as not scheduled work by user decision, authorization is RBAC with capability indirection plus opt-in ownership rather than a resource/attribute policy engine, and `SECURITY.md:81` lists vault binding as a consumer responsibility although the framework ships a Key Vault configuration helper.
   - _Remediation:_ Align `SECURITY.md:81` with the shipped `AddCommonKeyVaultConfiguration` helper (doc hygiene). No scheduled lever lifts the score: the threat model is an accepted, unscheduled gap.
   - _Expected delta:_ No index movement is scheduled; 8→9 (+3 weighted) would need the threat model and a resource/attribute policy engine.

5. **Production continuity evidence is consumer-owned**, §29 (maturity 4, impl 8, weight 3, implPriority 3): failure isolation is fitness-enforced and the in-repo restore drill is a build gate with a measured baseline, but the framework's own guide records production RTO/RPO, restores and SLO alerting as the consumer's (`Website/docs-src/guides/common-RESILIENCE.md:5,31`), with the SLO section an unfilled template (`:95`).
   - _Remediation:_ Tested restores against real cloud backups, RTO/RPO per service and measured production SLOs in the consumer IaC; nothing further is schedulable in-repo.
   - _Expected delta:_ +1 impl on §29 (+3 weighted) is a consumer-side lift.

## Top 5 strengths

1. **Dual-enforced Clean Architecture dependency rule (compile-time + fitness functions)**, §3 (impl 9): `Source/Build/MMCA.Common.LayerEnforcement.targets:1-90` fails the build on layer violation (`BeforeTargets ResolveProjectReferences`); `LayerDependencyTests.cs:12-58` and `DomainPurityTests.cs:13-26` re-enforce Shared→Domain→Application→Infra→API/UI and Domain framework-freedom via NetArchTest. Reinforced by §34 (impl 8).
   - _Remediation:_ Preserve by keeping both gates wired into CI and adding a regression test whenever a new layer/package is introduced; document the targets file in CLAUDE.md. To push the residual point, narrow the broader base-controller/UoW surfaces noted in §1.
   - _Expected delta:_ Preserves impl 9 on §3; protects §34 from regression.

2. **Reference-quality test strategy (define-once fitness functions + an enforced coverage gate)**, §14 (impl 9): a right-shaped pyramid (**~2,254** `[Fact]/[Theory]`) with executable architecture fitness functions (LayerDependency, DomainPurity, MicroserviceExtraction, DependencyVersion, PiiConvention, PiiErasureContract, AggregateConvention, EventVersioningConvention, FrameworkSanity, **Specification**, **SliceCohesion**) authored once in the shipped `MMCA.Common.Testing.Architecture` package (**138 methods across 53 abstract bases**) and re-run identically across all three repos (ADR-015), a zero-discovery min-tests guard, a shipped reusable E2E package, and a **68.3% line-coverage floor that fails the build** (`ci.yml:481`, measured ~70.3%).
   - _Remediation:_ Preserve by keeping the floor green and ratcheting it upward over releases; add mutation testing on the Core tier to reach a 10.
   - _Expected delta:_ Holds §14 at impl 9; mutation testing on the Core tier is the remaining path toward 10; protects §28.

3. **Supply-chain hardening with a license-pin build gate, a blocking SBOM, and Dependabot held-package guards**, §32 (impl 9, weight 3): `Directory.Build.props:8-10` (lock files, NuGetAudit, audit=all); `Directory.Packages.props:54-56` (MassTransit 8.5.5 pinned with documented v9 commercial-license crash hazard, ADR-016); `DependencyVersionTestsBase.cs:24-37` fails the build if any MassTransit package goes major≥9; 26 `packages.lock.json`; CycloneDX SBOM a hard release gate plus signed build-provenance attestations (`release.yml:82,91`); and a committed `.github/dependabot.yml:24-35` with `semver-major` ignores on every held package, so the pin discipline lives in config as well as the test.
   - _Remediation:_ Automate the coordinated consumer version sweep (currently policy) to close the one remaining minor gap.
   - _Expected delta:_ Automating the sweep nudges §32 toward fully exemplary; preserves impl 9.

4. **Centralized, alg-pinned security with shipped anti-automation defenses and PBKDF2 hashing**, §11 (impl 8): `WebApplicationBuilderExtensions.cs:364,380` pins ValidAlgorithms per RS256/HS256 (defeats alg-confusion) with full issuer/audience/lifetime/signing-key validation, `:217` extends the RS256 pin to the JWKS-forwarded path; `:34` rate-limit bypass for health/.well-known/grpc; `LoginProtectionService.cs:50-56,74-82` adds exponential brute-force lockout + per-IP registration limit (ADR-029); `PasswordHasher.cs:9,53` hashes with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512 (600k iterations, PBKDF2-only, `FixedTimeEquals`, ADR-032): all returning `Result.Failure` rather than throwing.
   - _Remediation:_ Keep the CI vulnerable-package gate green; align `SECURITY.md:81` with the shipped Key Vault helper.
   - _Expected delta:_ Preserves impl 8; the threat model that 8→9 needs is an accepted, unscheduled gap (see risk 4).

5. **Architecture-test-enforced microservices extraction boundaries + event versioning**, §7 (maturity 4, impl 9): ADR-006 (database-per-service), transport-agnostic `IMessageBus.cs:28`, and the shared `Rules/Layering/ArchitectureRules.Transport.cs:19` rule (subclassed through `Bases/Layering/MicroserviceExtractionTestsBase.cs:13` in `MicroserviceExtractionTests.cs:11`, run at `ci.yml:183` under a required check) forbidding Application/Domain/Shared→MassTransit and Grpc→Domain/Application/Infrastructure. Reinforced by §6's `BaseIntegrationEvent.SchemaVersion` (ADR-010) + `EventVersioningConventionTests`, so cross-service contracts carry an explicit version, and by ADR-090 upcasting for retired contracts. The rubric-v2 modernization-patterns criterion is met in shipped docs (`Grpc/DependencyInjection.cs:21,71,78`, `007-grpc-extraction.md:23`, `008-service-extraction-topology.md:41`).
   - _Remediation:_ Preserve the naming by keeping the ACL and Strangler Fig wording in the Grpc XML docs and in ADR-007/008 whenever either is revised (no drift gate watches the naming itself); to push the residual point, demonstrate one end-to-end lift-out (the Notifications module) in a sample to convert "extractable by design" into "extracted and proven".
   - _Expected delta:_ 9→10 on §7 needs a proven extraction, which is structurally consumer-side.
